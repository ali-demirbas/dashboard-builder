The mix effect is -0.086pp[21], 47%[177] of the -0.183pp change. Paid social took 98%[27] of the spend increase and was credited +34.2%[148] orders. Label driver: business impact (half the decline, tied to the spend increase); HIGH rather than CRITICAL because a channel-classification or tagging change for Paid social cannot be ruled out from this file. Measurement confidence: Medium (comparability CONDITIONAL — the rule assigning orders to channels is not stated). (see: CVR change decomposition; channel detail table)
  • HIGH · Unfavorable Paid search lost orders at flat spend. Orders 8,912 → 8,225 (-7.7%[144]), revenue -8.0%[145], spend +0.9%[146].
